1. Where we use AI

Digital Envisioned Toolbox uses artificial intelligence in some features. Depending on the page or service, this may include:

  • AI chat assistants that answer questions, collect your details, or help you book.
  • AI voice receptionists (powered by providers such as Vapi) that may answer or place phone calls on behalf of Digital Envisioned or a business that uses our service.
  • AI-generated or AI-assisted content, such as text, images, templates, or tool output.

2. AI phone calls and recording

When you speak with an AI receptionist, you are talking with an automated system, not a person, and the call will say so. Calls handled by the AI may be recorded and transcribed so the message can be passed on, the request completed, and the service improved. If you do not want to talk to the AI or be recorded, you may hang up and email us, or ask to leave a message for a person. We do not use an AI or prerecorded voice for telemarketing calls without your prior express written consent.

3. Notifications

After an AI conversation, a summary or alert may be sent by text message or email to the business owner who uses our service, and, if you asked for it or consented, to you.

4. Limits of AI

AI can make mistakes. Answers may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. AI output is not legal, financial, tax, medical, or other professional advice, and it is not a binding quote or commitment unless a person confirms it in writing. Please verify important information before relying on it.

5. Your information

What you type or say to an AI feature is processed by us and our AI service providers to provide the feature. We do not sell it. Please do not share sensitive information, such as payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or health details, in an AI chat or call. See our Privacy Policy.
