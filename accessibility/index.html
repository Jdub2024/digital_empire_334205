1. Our commitment

Digital Envisioned wants everyone to be able to use LexiVault Pro, including people with disabilities. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our design target.

2. What we do

  • Use semantic HTML, headings, and labels so screen readers can navigate.
  • Design mobile-first, with readable text sizes and strong color contrast.
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images where we can.

3. Current status

We have not completed a formal third-party accessibility audit, and some pages, older content, or third-party tools (such as payment forms or embedded widgets) may not yet fully meet our target. We are working to improve them.

4. Report a barrier or ask for help

If you have trouble using any part of this site, tell us and we will help, including by providing information or completing a purchase in another way. Email jnworkflow@gmail.com or call 205-578-8884. Please include the page address and a short description. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
