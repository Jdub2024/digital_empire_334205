1. Who we are

Wealth Doodle (go.digitalenvisioned.net) is operated by Digital Envisioned, a business owned by Joshua Newton and based in Alabama, United States. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Digital Envisioned. “You” means anyone who visits or uses this site.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: for example your name, email address, phone number, business name, and the contents of messages or forms you submit.
  • Automatically collected information: pages viewed, referring page, approximate device and browser type, and similar technical data, collected through our first-party analytics described below and through standard server logs kept by our hosting provider.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and support the site and the products or services you request.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To send transactional emails (such as receipts, downloads, and account notices) and, if you opt in, marketing emails you can unsubscribe from at any time.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • To keep the site secure, enforce our terms, and comply with law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the site, and only as needed for that work:

  • Hostinger: website hosting and email infrastructure.
  • Email service providers that send our emails and manage our mailing list.
  • Authorities or others when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use a first-party, cookieless analytics script (served from https://claw.digitalenvisioned.net/t.js) to count page views and understand which pages are useful. It does not set advertising cookies and is not used to track you across other companies’ websites. Some features, such as staying signed in or completing checkout, may use strictly necessary cookies. Payment pages hosted by Stripe or PayPal may set their own cookies. See our Cookie & Analytics Notice for details.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above: for example, for as long as your account or subscription is active, and afterward as needed for tax, accounting, dispute, and legal purposes. Marketing and SMS consent records are kept as proof of consent. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a data breach that affects you, we will notify you as required by applicable law, including the Alabama Data Breach Notification Act.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, deletion: email us to request a copy of your information, correct it, or delete it. We will verify your request and respond within a reasonable time, generally within 45 days.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email us.
  • State privacy rights: residents of some U.S. states have additional rights under their state’s privacy law. Where those laws apply to us, we will honor those rights. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.
  • Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control: because we do not sell personal information or use cross-site advertising tracking, there is nothing to opt out of; we treat these signals as a request not to sell or share.

9. Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the “Effective” date above and, for material changes, give notice on the site or by email where appropriate.
