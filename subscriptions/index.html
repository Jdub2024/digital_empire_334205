Subscription & Auto-Renewal Terms

Effective: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Applies to leads.digitalenvisioned.net

In short: subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel anytime online, by email, or by phone, and you keep access through the period you paid for.

1. Key terms shown before you buy

Before you start a subscription, the checkout page shows: the price and billing frequency, the length of any free trial or introductory price and what you will pay when it ends, that the subscription renews automatically until you cancel, and how to cancel. We ask for your express consent to these terms before charging you, and we email you a confirmation with the same information.

2. Automatic renewal

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period (for example, monthly or yearly) and your payment method on file is charged the then-current price plus any applicable tax, until you cancel. For annual plans, we send a reminder before the renewal date.

3. Free trials and introductory prices

If your plan starts with a free trial or introductory price, it converts to the regular price shown at checkout when the trial or introductory period ends unless you cancel before then. Where a trial lasts longer than 7 days, we send a reminder before the first regular charge.

4. How to cancel

Cancelling is as easy as signing up. You can cancel at any time by any of these methods:

  • Online, from your account or billing page where available (for example, a “Manage billing” or “Cancel subscription” link);
  • By emailing jnworkflow@gmail.com with the subject “Cancel” and the email address on the subscription; or
  • By calling 205-578-8884.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep access until then. We will confirm your cancellation by email. We will not require you to talk to a salesperson or accept an offer in order to cancel.

5. Price changes

If we change the price of your subscription, we will notify you in advance, at least 30 days before the change takes effect for your next renewal, so you can cancel if you do not want to continue.

See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
