1. Our analytics is cookieless

We measure site traffic with a first-party script we run ourselves (https://claw.digitalenvisioned.net/t.js). It does not set cookies or use similar device identifiers, and it does not follow you to other companies’ websites. It records basic information such as the page you viewed, the referring page, the time, and general device and browser type, so we can tell which pages are helpful. Any network information used to deliver the page (such as your IP address) is not used to build an advertising profile of you.

3. Your controls

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop sign-in or checkout from working. Because we do not use advertising cookies, there is no ad-tracking opt-out to set.
