ACH Debit Authorization

Effective: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Applies to 3d.digitalenvisioned.net

In short: by choosing bank payment you allow us, through Stripe, to debit your account for the amounts and schedule shown at checkout. You can revoke this at any time with 3 business days’ notice.

1. What this authorization covers

When you choose to pay by bank account (ACH Direct Debit) at checkout, you authorize Digital Envisioned, through its payment processor Stripe, to electronically debit the bank account you provide for the amount shown at checkout and, if you are buying a subscription or an installment plan, for each later payment on the schedule shown at checkout. If a debit is made in error, you authorize a correcting credit or debit for the same amount.

At checkout you will see the amount, the date or frequency of payments, and an on-screen mandate that you accept by clicking to confirm. Stripe or we will send you a confirmation of the authorization by email.

2. Amounts and timing

  • One-time payment: a single debit for the checkout total.
  • Recurring payment: a debit each billing period for the plan price plus any applicable tax, on or about the renewal date.
  • If the amount of a recurring debit will change, we will notify you at least 10 days before the debit.
  • ACH payments can take up to several business days to clear. Access to a product may begin when the payment is initiated or when it clears, as stated at checkout.

3. How to cancel this authorization

You may revoke this authorization at any time by emailing jnworkflow@gmail.com or calling 205-578-8884, or by cancelling your subscription. Please allow at least 3 business days before the next scheduled debit for the revocation to take effect. Revoking the authorization does not cancel amounts already owed.

4. Returned payments

If a debit is returned (for example, for insufficient funds), we may retry it once, pause access until payment is made, or ask you to use another payment method. We do not charge a return fee unless one is disclosed at checkout and permitted by law. Your bank may charge its own fees.

5. Your rights

You confirm that you are an authorized signer on the account. Under U.S. rules for electronic payments, you may have the right to dispute an unauthorized debit with your bank. For business accounts, the time allowed to dispute may be shorter. Please contact us first so we can help.
