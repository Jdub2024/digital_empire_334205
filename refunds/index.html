Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Applies to software.digitalenvisioned.net

1. Digital products

Because digital products are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, sales are generally final once the product has been delivered or downloaded. We will gladly fix or refund a purchase if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same item;
  • the file or access link does not work and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time; or
  • the product is materially different from its description.

If a specific product page offers a money-back guarantee, the terms stated on that page apply to that product.

2. Services

For services, you may cancel for a full refund before work has begun. Once work has started, refunds are prorated for work not yet performed, unless a written agreement or the product page says otherwise.

3. How to request a refund

Email jnworkflow@gmail.com with your name, the email used for the purchase, the order or receipt number, and the reason. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; card refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days and bank (ACH) refunds within about 3–7 business days, depending on your bank.

4. Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank or card issuer; most problems can be fixed quickly. Your rights under applicable law and your card or bank agreement are not affected by this policy.
